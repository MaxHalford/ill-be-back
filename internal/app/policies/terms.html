I’ll Be Back is a tool operated by Max Halford to announce your availability across apps. Contact maxhalford25@gmail.com for help.

Using the service

Connect only accounts you are allowed to manage. You authorize the app to apply the changes you request. Your provider’s own terms and workplace policies also apply. Do not use the service for abuse or to access someone else’s account.

How changes work

Switching away replaces the relevant status or vacation message. Switching back clears the Slack and GitHub statuses, disables Gmail vacation replies and deletes the Calendar event created here. Previous statuses and vacation messages are not restored.

Calendar requires a return time and a Google account that supports out-of-office events. Other apps stay away until you switch back. The dashboard shows the last confirmed update; changes made directly in another app may not be reflected here. Provider outages, revoked permissions or account restrictions can prevent an update. Check any reported errors.

Leaving

You can disconnect apps or delete your account in Account & settings. Deleting your account removes data stored here but leaves any external statuses or events in place. Clear those before deleting or directly in the provider. See our privacy policy for details.

Availability and changes

This is an early service. Features may change and continuous availability is not guaranteed. There are currently no paid subscriptions. Any future paid plan will show its price and terms before you choose it.
